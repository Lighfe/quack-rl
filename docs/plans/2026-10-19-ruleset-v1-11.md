Spec: `docs/specs/2026-10-19-ruleset-v1-11.md`. Recipes: skill `quack_rule_changes` (use it with a grain of salt, and improve it). The order follows the v1.10 stage.

One stage, eight sub-issues in this order. Each one ends with the full test command green and the older versions unchanged.

1. Docs cleanup (doc only): existing #139 (reword the colour count sentence), the two stale test names in `colour-change.md` and `shop-change.md`, and the Status line of `docs/process.md` (#130, folded into this issue).
2. Ruleset v1.11 file, the `cards` field with its validation, the 12 cards as components, rules document.
3. Engine A: deck state, the draw through the chance source, the round-start step, the cards without a choice (1, 3, 4, 7) and the no-effect cards, events, record and replay test.
4. Engine B: the joint card choice (cards 5, 6) and the restart of card 2 in the middle of the brew, events, record and replay test.
5. Bots, balance report (explosion limit of the round, restart), tournament.
6. CLI: the card on the board, the cards left, the choice prompts, the restart question.
7. RL: stored sample of layouts 1 to 5 first, then layout 6, the new actions, environments, refusal of trained policies.
8. v1.11 becomes the default: defaults and their test, README, milestone row, skill update.

Blockers: each of 3 to 8 is blocked by the one before it. Task 2 is blocked by nothing and task 3 by task 2. Task 1 is free.

Not in this stage: the points board with rat tails and the rat tail (v1.12, a new intake, tracked in #79), a second speed-up of the test suite (after v2), self-play (#84, #86, #85).

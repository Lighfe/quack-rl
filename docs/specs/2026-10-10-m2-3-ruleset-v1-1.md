# M2.3: ruleset v1.1 and a small tournament

Date: 2026-10-10. Follows stage #35 (first balance run, `docs/analysis/2026-10-balance-v1.md`).

## Purpose

Apply the owner's rule decisions from the v1 balance run as a new ruleset `v1.1`, adapt the bots with the smallest change that follows the new shop rule, and run a small tournament whose per-round money and points (snowball behaviour), end bags, ranking and end points the owner can explore in a marimo notebook.

## Owner's reading of the v1 run (context, not tasks)

- The analysis lacks money available and points total per round. These show snowball behaviour and are the most important new data.
- The `cleaner` bot ending with 3.87 White 1 on average shows that the bot did not work as meant: it is an Orange 1 printing machine, and that seems to be the winning line. It is not a sign that White removal is too cheap or too strong.
- Almost none of the winning bots bought a 4-value chip.
- Buying points is not fun as a design (chips for about 3 rounds, then mostly points, no real snowball). This is a major change and is NOT part of this stage; the RL environment comes first.
- Bot programming with clear rules is hard. Do not spend much effort on tuning bots; a quickly tuned RL agent will likely do better later.

## Decisions

### Ruleset v1.1

- New file `src/quack_rl/rules/v1.1.toml` with `version = "v1.1"`, and the human-readable rules `docs/rules/rules-v1.1.md`. `v1.toml` does not change (it has recorded games). `v1.1` is a supported rules version.
- Everything not named here is the same as `v1`.
- The shop item `droplet_1` ("advance droplet by 1") is removed. The bonus die keeps its droplet faces (`droplet_1`, `droplet_half`).
- Prices: `points_2` 6 → 7, `points_10` 22 → 21, `green_2` 8 → 9, `blue_2` 9 → 8, `green_4` 14 → 13, `blue_4` 16 → 15, `remove_white_1` 15 → 14. Unchanged: `orange_1` 3, `blue_1` 5, `green_1` 4, `points_5` 13.
- New shop rule:
  - A player buys at most 2 chip items (`kind = "chip"`) per round, and the two must have different colours.
  - Points items and `remove_white_1` have no count limit. Only money limits them (and, for `remove_white_1`, the White 1 chips the player owns).
  - The shop phase of a player ends when the player chooses `done`. There is no other purchase cap in `v1.1`.
- The rule is ruleset data, so `v1` keeps `max_purchases = 3` and plays exactly as before (same seeds, same results). Keys: `max_purchases` becomes optional (absent = no overall cap); new optional `max_chip_purchases` (absent = no chip cap) and `distinct_chip_colours` (default `false`). `v1.1` sets `max_chip_purchases = 2`, `distinct_chip_colours = true` and no `max_purchases`.
- `quack-rl report` and the realistic baseline maximum work on a ruleset without `max_purchases` (points items then have no count limit, only money).

### Bots

- From the points round on: buy only points items, the biggest one the bot can pay, again and again until no points item is affordable, then `done`. (As in v1, but now with no cap of 3.)
- Before the points round:
  1. Buy from the strategy list (best first) while an item of the list is legal and affordable.
  2. When no item of the list is legal and affordable, buy the most expensive legal and affordable item that is not a points item (this can be a chip of another colour, or `remove_white_1`). Repeat.
  3. When nothing is left: `done`. Money left over is lost at the round end, as now.
- Items of a strategy list that are not in the shop of the ruleset (the droplet in `v1.1`) are skipped.
- The bot names and the strategy lists stay. No tuning.
- Step 2 applies only when the ruleset sets `max_chip_purchases`. On `v1` the bots play the same games as before.

### Tournament output

- `results.csv` gets new per-game columns, for each seat (`p1_`, `p2_`):
  - `<seat>_money_r<k>` for each round k = 1..rounds: the money of the seat when its shop phase of round k starts (after the die and the last-round bonus);
  - `<seat>_points_r<k>` for each round k: the points total of the seat at the end of round k (after the shop);
  - `<seat>_bag_<chip>` for each chip id of the ruleset: the count of that chip the seat owns at game end (bag plus placed chips).
- They are filled for every run and do not need `--keep-records`. The averages per bot (over all its games, both seats) are computed by the notebook.
- New named field `small`: draw limits 10 and 20, points rounds 4 and 6, strategies blue, green, cleaner, balanced (16 bots), plus the baseline `draw20-pts1-points` = 17 bots, 136 pairings. `full` stays as it is.

### Run and result

- One run: `quack-rl tournament --field small --rules v1.1 --games 200 --seed <fixed> --workers 8 --name <name>` (27,200 games). Plus the seat check of the small field, as in v1, before the main run. No sweep.
- A marimo notebook in `notebooks/` (marimo as a dev dependency) that takes a run folder and shows:
  - the ranking: win rate (a draw counts half) with a 95% interval, and average end points per bot;
  - the end bag per bot;
  - two line charts: average money per round and average points total per round, one line per bot, with a multi-select filter of the bots (default: all).
- A short note `docs/analysis/2026-10-balance-v1.1.md`: setup (machine, commit, commands, seeds, wall clock), seat check result, ranking with average end points, end bags, and a pointer to the notebook. No findings section.
- The raw run stays in `data/` (not committed).

# Ruleset v1.8: field points and explosion choice

Status: draft for the owner's approval. Date: 2026-10-16.

## Intent

The owner found that rules v1.1 did not become a fun game: points come only from the shop, so the game is "chips for about 3 rounds, then mostly buying points", with no real snowball (see `docs/specs/2026-10-10-m2-3-ruleset-v1-1.md` and issue #79). v1.8 is a first step towards v2, not v2 itself. It changes how points are earned and what an explosion costs. The colour effects (all colours, spellbooks) come in later stages.

The label "1.8" is the owner's. It shows "almost v2". The ruleset id is `v1.8`.

## Goals

1. Points come from the track: every field has a point value, next to its money. A potion that does not explode gives both.
2. The shop sells no points. Chips and "Remove one White 1" stay.
3. An explosion halves nothing. The player who explodes chooses: the money of the scoring field, or its points.
4. The bots decide the explosion choice by round. The old bot names keep their meaning (below).
5. The RL environments run on v1.8 (no retrain).
6. Notebooks and everything else that uses a ruleset stay on v1.1 explicitly.
7. A project skill `rule_changes` records how to change the rules in this repo.

## Non-goals

- New colours, colour effects, spellbooks (#71). They are the next stage.
- Retraining a policy on v1.8. Policies and datasets made on v1.1 stay valid for v1.1 only.
- Fixing the legacy notebooks beyond pinning them to v1.1.
- Balance tuning of v1.8.

## Anti-goals

- Changing the behaviour of `v1` or `v1.1`. All their games, records, datasets and tests stay valid and unchanged.
- Silent changes: the action space and the observation of v1.1 stay as they are.

## Rules of v1.8 (changes from v1.1)

Everything not named here is as in v1.1 (`docs/rules/rules-v1.1.md`): chips, track, rubies, droplet, bonus die, brewing, shop prices, 9 rounds, end of game.

### Points per field

Every field `0..53` has a point value `points[field]`, as `money[field]`. The scoring field gives money and points (the scoring field is as in v1.1: the first free field after the last placed chip).

Known from the owner (board photo): the last field gives 15 points; the fields with money 27 (fields 39, 40) give 10; with money 22 (fields 29, 30) give 7; with money 17 (fields 19, 20) give 4.

My guess (to be corrected by the owner later): the other fields follow the same line, `points = max(0, round(0.6 * money - 6.2))`. It matches all four known values. The table is written out in full in `v1.8.toml`, so a correction is an edit of numbers.

### End of a round

Resolve is as in v1.1 (ruby, green bonus, bonus die), except:

- A potion that did **not** explode gives the money of the scoring field and the points of the scoring field. The bonus die money +1 applies to money as in v1.1. There is no last-round money factor in v1.8 (`last_round_money_percent = 100`): the owner replaced it with the last-round rule below.
- A potion that **exploded** gives nothing yet. Both explosion effects of v1.1 are gone (no halving). The player chooses (next section).
- The bonus die face "+1 victory point" stays. An exploded player never rolls, as in v1.1.

### Last round (round 9)

Money is turned into points. All money a player receives in round 9 (field money, the bonus die +1, or `take_money` after an explosion) becomes `money // 5` points (rounded down) at once, and the player keeps no money. Points from the field (or `take_points`) are added as in other rounds.

- So after an explosion in round 9, `take_money` gives `money[field] // 5` points and `take_points` gives `points[field]`.
- The shop of round 9 then has no money to spend. My call: the builder may skip the round 9 shop phase if that keeps the code and records simpler, or keep it with everyone choosing Done. A bot never needs to buy in round 9.

### Explosion choice

A new step between Resolve and Shop, only when at least one potion exploded. Each player who exploded chooses `take_money` or `take_points`, at the same time and hidden, as the Draw or Stop choice. A player who did not explode waits.

- `take_money`: the money of the scoring field, in full (no halving).
- `take_points`: the points of the scoring field. The player gets no money this round.
- The ruby and green droplet bonus after an explosion stays as in v1.1.

### Shop

The items "2 victory points", "5 victory points" and "10 victory points" are removed. All other items and prices are as in v1.1. Chip limits (2 chips per round, different colours) are as in v1.1.

## Bots

The names stay `draw<percent>-pts<round>-<strategy>`. The meaning of `pts<round>` changes for v1.8:

- v1 and v1.1: unchanged (buy points from that round on).
- v1.8: when the bot explodes, it chooses money in the rounds before `<round>` and points from `<round>` on. Example: `pts4` chooses money in round 3 and points in round 4.
- v1.8: the bot never tries to buy points (there are none). In the shop it follows its strategy as in the v1.1 fallback.
- The `random` bot picks any legal action.

This is the "point philosophy": a bot that goes for points late builds its bag first.

## Game record and engine

- The choice is a normal action in the record, so a replay works as before. v1 and v1.1 records are unchanged and keep loading.
- The ruleset decides whether the choice step exists (my call: a switch in the ruleset file, `explosion_choice = true`). Without it the engine behaves as v1.1.
- Point values per field are a ruleset table, loaded and checked like `money` (one entry per field).

## RL environments

- The action space of a v1.8 environment has two more actions. The environment, the action mask, the observation layout and the tournament run on v1.8. The layout gets a new version (layout 3; layout 2 belongs to v1.1). Policies trained on v1.1 are not loaded on v1.8.
- The observation of v1.1 does not change.
- Reward: unchanged (win or loss at the end).
- The check is a short test run per environment with the bots and a random policy on v1.8. No training run.

## Legacy

The notebooks (`notebooks/`) and the CLI defaults stay on v1.1 or v1, set explicitly. They must not pick up v1.8 by accident. Where a default `rules` value exists, it is named in a test.

## The `rule_changes` skill

It lists: the rules document, the ruleset file and its model, the engine, the record schema and replay, the bots, the RL layers, the tests and the notebooks; the rule "never edit a version with recorded games, make a new file"; and a checklist for a new ruleset version.

# Ruleset v1.11: round-start cards

Status: draft for the owner's approval. Date: 2026-10-19.

## Intent

v1.10 added three colours and a minimum buy round. v1.11 adds the first part of v2 (issue #79): a random card at the start of each round. The card changes the round for all players, or gives each player a choice. The owner's reason: the game did not become "fun"; cards make rounds differ from each other and give small decisions.

v1.11 has 12 cards. The real game has 20 cards; the other cards come later. The points board with rat tails and the rat tail catch-up mechanic are not part of v1.11. They come as v1.12 (a separate intake, tracked in #79), because they change the scoring and interact with card 7.

## Goals

1. Ruleset `v1.11`: v1.10 plus a deck of 12 round-start cards.
2. Engine, record and replay, bots, report, CLI and the RL environments run on v1.11.
3. v1.11 becomes the default `rules` value of the code (the same step as v1.10 had, last issue of the stage).
4. The skill `quack_rule_changes` is brought up to date.

## Non-goals

- The points board, rat tails, the rat tail (v1.12).
- The other 8 cards of the real game, and balance work on the cards.
- Retraining a policy on v1.11.
- Changing any older version.

## Anti-goals

- Changing the behaviour of `v1`, `v1.1`, `v1.8`, `v1.9` or `v1.10`. Their games, records, datasets and tests stay valid.
- A hidden choice leaking to the other seat before it resolves, a hard-coded colour count, or a new action inserted into the middle of the action list (known mistakes in the skill).
- Randomness outside the chance source (the replay must be exact).

## Words

- Drop: the droplet position of one player. "Drop +1" is `droplet_halves += 2`, "+1.5" is three, "+0.5" is one, "+2" is four. Each player has their own drop. Placing chips never moves the drop; it moves at round end, by a card or by the shop. (The owner's word "drop".)
- Pot: the chips a player has placed this round.
- Explosion limit: `explosion_limit` of the ruleset, today 7: the potion explodes when the white total is more than the limit. The owner says it as "explodes at 8", so "8 to 10" in card 4 means the limit goes from 7 to 9.
- Points: the normal game points of the player, the total so far.

## The deck

New ruleset field `cards`: a list of entries, each with `id` (string), `effect` (string, the name of a registered card component) and `count` (integer, at least 1). Default: empty list, which means the game has no cards and older versions play as before.

The v1.11 deck, 12 cards:

| id | effect | count |
|---|---|---|
| card_1 | ruby step +1 instead of +0.5 | 1 |
| card_2 | restart option after 5 chips | 1 |
| card_3 | every drop +1 | 1 |
| card_4 | explosion limit +2 this round | 1 |
| card_5 | choice: black 1 chip, or any value-2 chip, or drop +1.5 at round end | 1 |
| card_6 | choice: drop +2, or purple 1 chip | 1 |
| card_7 | fewest points get a green 1 chip | 1 |
| card_8 to card_12 | no effect | 5 (one entry `no_effect` with count 5 is also fine; builder's call) |

Draw: at the start of every round, one card is drawn at random from the cards that have not been drawn yet in this game (without replacement). The 9 rounds use 9 of the 12 cards; 3 are never drawn. The draw goes through the chance source and appears in the chance log, so replay works. The state keeps the cards still in the deck and the card of the current round. (My call: draw uniformly from the remaining cards each round instead of shuffling once. The result is the same and the log is simple.)

Information: the drawn card is open for both players at once, before anyone acts in the round. The list of drawn cards, and so the cards left in the deck, is open too.

## Round order with cards

1. Round start: draw the card. Immediate effects resolve (cards 3 and 7, and a drop or chip that follows from a choice at once).
2. If the card is a choice card (5, 6): both players choose at the same moment, each for themselves, without seeing the other pick. Then the choices resolve.
3. Brew, resolve, choice (explosion), shop: as in v1.10, with the card effects below.

The step for the card draw takes no action (like the resolve step). The step for the card choice is a joint action, one action per seat, like the explosion choice of v1.8.

## The cards

All effects are per player: own pot, own drop, own bag.

### Card 1: ruby

At round end, a player whose scoring field is a ruby field gets drop +1 instead of +0.5 (two half steps instead of one). The ruby condition is the existing one. Only the ruby step changes. Money and points of the field do not.

### Card 2: restart

Once per round, per player: after the 5th chip of the pot has been placed, that player chooses:

- (A) continue as normal.
- (B) restart: all 5 placed chips go back into the bag, the bag is reshuffled, the field goes back to the player's start field, the white total and the explosion total go back to 0, and the pot is empty. The player brews on from there. Nothing of the 5 chips is kept. This is not like the yellow put-back.

My calls:

- The choice is offered only if the player is still brewing after the 5th chip: not exploded, not stopped. A player who exploded on the 5th chip cannot restart.
- A player gets the choice once; after a restart (B) or after (A) there is no second choice, even if the pot again reaches 5 chips.
- The other player can be at any point of the brew. A player who has not reached 5 chips just plays on; the joint action has exactly one action per seat, so the engine needs a waiting action for the seat that does not decide.
- Chip effects that fired when the 5 chips were placed (for example a hand of blue chips, or yellow put-backs) are all undone with the reset, since the chips are back in the bag. Blue chips in the hand go back to the bag as well (builder: check what the hand holds at that moment).
- Round-end effects (green, black, purple, ruby) look at the pot at round end, so they see only the chips placed after the restart.

### Card 3: drop +1

Every player's drop moves +1, right away at round start.

### Card 4: explosion limit

For this round only, the explosion limit is the ruleset limit plus 2 (7 becomes 9: with a white total of 10 you explode, with 9 you do not). It applies to both players and to every place that reads the limit (the brew step, the bot look-ahead, the report, the RL bounds and observation). It is back to normal in the next round.

### Card 5: choice

Each player chooses one of:

- (A) a black 1 chip goes into the bag.
- (B) any chip with value 2 goes into the bag. The player names the chip. The choices are all chips of the ruleset with value 2 (builder: list them from the ruleset, not by hand). My call: the minimum buy round does not limit this choice.
- (C) at round end, the player's drop moves +1.5.

A chip goes into the bag at once and can be drawn in the same round. Option (C) fires at round end even if the pot exploded (my call, as for ruby, black and purple).

### Card 6: choice

Each player chooses one of:

- (A) drop +2, right away.
- (B) a purple 1 chip goes into the bag, at once.

### Card 7: fewest points

The player with the fewest points gets a green 1 chip in the bag, right away. If several players have the fewest points, they all get it. In round 1 everybody has 0 points, so both players get it (the owner confirmed this).

### Cards with no effect

Nothing happens in that round.

## Order of round-end effects

At round end, per player: ruby (card 1 changes the amount), green, black, purple, then the drop of card 5 (C). All of them only add to the drop, so the order does not change the result. Keep the order for the events in the record.

## Game record and engine

- The draw is a chance outcome. The card, the choices and the restart are events or actions in the record, so replay and `verify --seed-check` work. The record format is generic; no schema change is expected.
- New state: the deck (cards left), the current card, the choices made, whether a player has used the restart this round, and the round-end drop that card 5 (C) promised.
- The new action names are appended at the end of the action list (the order is the order of the RL action mask).

## Bots

Every bot answers every action the engine offers. The heuristic bot needs a rule for the card choice (card 5, 6) and for the restart (card 2). A simple rule is enough; balance is not a goal. The look-ahead of the explosion total uses the limit of the round (card 4). The random bot needs nothing.

## CLI

The board shows the card of the round, and the cards left in the deck. Both players are asked for the card choice. A human sees only their own pick until both have chosen. The restart question is asked when the pot reaches 5 chips.

## RL environments

Observation layout 6 shows: the card of the round (one entry per card kind, including no effect), the cards left in the deck (counts), the explosion limit of the round, whether the restart is available to the player, and the open information about the choices after they resolved. The opponent's pending choice is not in the observation. The action list grows by the card choice actions and the restart actions, appended at the end. The stored sample of layouts 1 to 5 is made first, before the encoder changes. No retrain; trained policies refuse v1.11 as they refuse v1.10.

## Defaults

The last issue makes v1.11 the default `rules` value everywhere, as in the v1.10 stage: the defaults test, README, the milestone row (and the note that v1.12 comes before M3), and the skill text. Notebooks stay pinned.

## The skill is a guide, not a law
